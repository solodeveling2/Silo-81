JUNK TOWN — Tripo Agentic Canvas brief
Reference: art/concepts/junk_town_ref.png

Design Junk Town, a circular fortified scavenger settlement in a frozen wasteland,
for a Fallout-style isometric RPG.

The perimeter is the signature: a ring wall built from rusted abandoned car bodies
placed nose-to-tail, combined with corrugated metal sheets, timber posts, scrap
plating and chain-link fence filling the gaps. Corner watchtowers made of
scaffolding and car parts.

Inside: patched shacks, workshops, a central muddy yard, animal pens with goats,
scrap piles, barrels, smoke from stovepipes, improvised power lines, snow on roofs
and car hoods.

Make it believable and improvised, built by survivors from junk - not a military
fortress. Realistic 3D game asset style, low-poly friendly, muted industrial palette
with rust and faded paint.

Deliver modular assets: car-wall section, corrugated fence section, junk gate,
watchtower, shack, animal pen.
