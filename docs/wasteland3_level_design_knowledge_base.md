## Summary of Core Design Principles for Silo 81
1. **5 Spatial Topologies**:
   - *Linear Hook with Sabotage Loop* (`Ambush Site`, `Garden of the Gods`, `Machine Shop`, `Hoon Homestead`)
   - *Mutating Social Hub* (`Ranger HQ`, `Downtown Colorado Springs`, `Little Vegas` -> `Closed Little Vegas`, `Bizarre Interior`)
   - *Asymmetric Ring / 3-Key Dungeon* (`Warrens`, `Monster Army Bunker`, `Old Survivalist Bunker`, `Aspen`)
   - *Combined-Arms Exterior Yard + Infantry Gate Cabins* (`Snowed Inn Resort`, `Aspen`, `Tellurium Mine`, `Knox Bison Ranch`)
   - *Zero-Combat Atmospheric Micro-Sites* (`Mysterious Cave`, `Abandoned Oil Well`, `Massacre Site`, `Toaster Repair Academy`)
2. **The "Pre-Combat Sabotage Apron"**:
   - Every major combat arena has an outer perimeter with **3 entry vectors**:
     1. Frontal Choke (0 skill required, high-risk or social `Kiss Ass`/`Hard Ass` check).
     2. Elevation Flank (`Ladder` / ridge for sniper overwatch).
     3. Sabotage Backdoor (`Mechanics` generator to disable turrets, `Nerd Stuff` computer to flip turrets/drones, `Lockpicking`/`Sneaky Shit` rear entry).
   - Include an **Environmental Opening Shot** (e.g., freezing water pipe in `Denver Ruins`, solar lens in `Garden of the Gods`, overhead crane in `Machine Shop`, liquid nitrogen tanks in `Sans Luxe`).
3. **Squad-Split Spatial Puzzles**:
   - Live-power pressure plates requiring one character to stand on a pad while another uses a terminal/door (`Garden of the Gods`, `Sans Luxe`, `Clown Museum`).
   - Transmitter/receiver separation (`Paint Mines` hilltop antenna + RV radio).
   - Trace-the-cable spark conduits (`Downtown`, `Union Station` 1-2-3-4 switches).
4. **Three-Horizon Exploration Loops**:
   - *Horizon 1 (Immediate Room)*: Safe codes, turret passwords, medkits/ammo on the sabotage flank.
   - *Horizon 2 (Permanent Build)*: Skill books, passive-buff collectibles, audio logs with spoken codes.
   - *Horizon 3 (Cross-Map Meta)*: Multi-map keys and artifacts that pay off hours later.
